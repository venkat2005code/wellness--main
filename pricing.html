We employ strict HIPAA Safe-Harbor de-identification protocols. Employer-facing HR dashboards only display aggregated population data for cohort sizes with a minimum threshold of 25 individuals. No HR administrator, broker, or executive ever has access to identifiable medical screenings, biometric numbers, or individual telehealth discussions.
Absolutely. Over 85% of our enterprise clients deploy VitalPulse in direct concert with their insurance brokers, captives, or self-insured Third-Party Administrators (TPAs). We provide actuarial data feeds that validate preventive claims suppression, allowing brokers to negotiate lower medical stop-loss renewal premiums on your behalf.
For on-site blood chemistry panels and biometric stations, our traveling clinical squads require a minimum of 50 participating lives per facility location. For multi-site corporations with smaller branch offices, we supply convenient home blood spot test kits or vouchers to local Quest Diagnostics / Labcorp patient service centers at identical tier pricing.
Standard deployment for our Essential and Comprehensive packages takes between 10 to 14 business days. Pre-built connectors for Workday, ADP, and BambooHR streamline automated employee eligibility rosters. For Enterprise 360 plans involving custom campus logistics, our change-management team leads a full 30-day staggered onboarding campaign with customized launch collateral.
Our Actuarial SLA is bound to self-funded enterprise clients with 1,500+ eligible employees. An independent accredited actuarial firm evaluates baseline year claims versus program participation over a 24-month horizon. If the calculated medical claims deflection does not match or exceed 2.5 times the PEPM investment fees, VitalPulse refunds the shortfall directly into your benefits treasury.
